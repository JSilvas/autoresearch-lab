# 2026031794_z_loss

**Hypothesis**: Z-loss (auxiliary logit regularization from PaLM) penalizes logit magnitude: z_loss = eps * mean(logsumexp(logits)^2). This discourages logit explosion/divergence by adding a gradient proportional to 2*eps*logsumexp*softmax(logit_k). With Z_LOSS_COEFF=1e-4, contribution ≈ 0.002-0.010 at typical logit scales (logsumexp≈5-10). Acts as regularization beyond weight decay: pushes logit distribution toward lower entropy/magnitude. We've been stuck at 1.369316 for 18 experiments — z-loss is a novel untried regularization axis.
**Target Metric**: val_bpb < 1.369316
**Code Change**: Add Z_LOSS_COEFF=1e-4 hyperparameter. In forward(), add z_loss = Z_LOSS_COEFF * logits.logsumexp(dim=-1).pow(2).mean() to cross-entropy loss. Revert Block.forward to sequential (from Exp93 parallel).
