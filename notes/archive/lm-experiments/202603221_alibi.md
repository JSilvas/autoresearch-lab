# 202603221_alibi

**Hypothesis**: ALiBi (Attention with Linear Biases) may outperform RoPE for our windowed-attention setup. ALiBi adds a distance-proportional negative bias to attention logits (no learned parameters beyond slopes), which naturally penalizes long-range attention and integrates with the window structure. RoPE at base=10000 is calibrated for seq_len=2048 but is purely rotation-based.
**Target Metric**: val_bpb < 1.368062 (best from mar17)
**Code Change**: `USE_ALIBI = True` (replaces RoPE in CausalSelfAttention; slopes = 2^(-8h/n_head) for h=1..n_head)

---
**Result**: 1.643088 (Δ+0.275 vs RoPE best 1.368062)
**Git Hash**: aefbede
**Verdict**: DISCARD — Catastrophically worse. ALiBi with hard window cut-off is a terrible combination: the distance bias fights with the hard window boundary and the fixed slopes (2^{-4}, 2^{-8} for 2 heads) are poorly calibrated for this architecture. Consistent with mar17 "No RoPE" result (+0.263); ALiBi is effectively another flavor of broken positional encoding here. RoPE at base=10000 remains essential.
