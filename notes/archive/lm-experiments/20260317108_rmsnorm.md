# 20260317108_rmsnorm

**Hypothesis**: RMSNorm (no mean-centering) may suit ReLU² activations better than LayerNorm. Mean-centering in LayerNorm is redundant if activations are naturally near-zero-mean; removing it avoids potential centering artifacts and is slightly cheaper.
**Target Metric**: val_bpb < 1.368062
**Code Change**: `norm()` → `F.rms_norm(x, (x.size(-1),))` (was `F.layer_norm`). Stateless in both cases, no MPS backward risk.

---
**Result**: 1.393843 (Δ+0.026 vs best 1.368062)
**Git Hash**: 15306eb
**Verdict**: DISCARD — RMSNorm dramatically worse. Mean-centering is load-bearing: ReLU² activations are always non-negative (non-zero mean), so removing centering causes persistent positive mean drift in the residual stream. LayerNorm confirmed necessary.
